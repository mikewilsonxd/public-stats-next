# T-54M

## Description

The **T-54M** is a Main Battle Tank representing a deep modernization of the T-54, bringing the old hull up to late-Cold War standards. Protection Is doubled with newly Added ERA which is at the front and and side turret and on the front hull 
The crew of four operates a stabilized **100mm D-10T** cannon paired with a laser rangefinder, FCS with lead computation, and 5x–10x zoom. Secondary armament includes a coaxial **7.62mm PKT** and a roof **12.7mm DSHK** the driver also has a **7.62 PKT**. The T-54M has no APS but benefits from modern optics and stabilizer for accurate fire on the move, extending the platform's usefulness into the modern era.
